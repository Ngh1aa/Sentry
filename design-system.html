Sentry/Design System
01 / 05

Clinical risk operations

High density without decision ambiguity.

Sentry uses a clinical light interface to help analysts correlate evidence quickly while keeping irreversible decisions explicit. The system favors dense information, strong grouping, persistent risk context and audit-ready rationale over decorative dashboard patterns.

DirectionClinical · precise · forensic
Core userFraud / risk analyst
SignatureSplit Investigation Workspace

01.04

Risk language

Every color-coded state also has a textual label. Risk meaning must never depend on hue alone.

Critical

HIGH RISK

Composite score above review threshold or severe correlated evidence.

Conflict

RULE CONFLICT

Automation policies disagree and manual precedence is required.

Verification

NEEDS VERIFICATION

Decision is deferred into a step-up identity challenge.

Resolved

ALLOWED

Review is closed with rationale retained in the audit trail.

01.06

Component inventory

Sentry uses visual telemetry only when it increases forensic comprehension; decorative charting is excluded.

QUEUE

Alert row

Risk score, reason, customer, age, selection and SLA context.

EVIDENCE

Diagram card

Risk gauge, velocity spline, geo hop, device comparison, entity graph and evidence trace.

DECISION

Decision action

Block, allow, verify, escalate and false-positive paths with rationale capture.

01.07

Motion & accessibility principles

Motion can reveal change, but it cannot delay or obscure an analyst decision.

Motion

State change, not spectacle

Animate queue selection, risk gauge updates and auto-advance subtly. Respect prefers-reduced-motion.

Keyboard

Visible focus

Queue items, tabs and decision actions need an obvious focus indicator and predictable order.

Semantics

Text before color

Critical, conflict, verification, frozen and resolved states remain legible without color perception.

Evidence boundary
