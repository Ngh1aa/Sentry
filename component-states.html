02.03

Investigation states

The workspace should preserve context while the underlying status changes.

HIGH_RISK

Immediate review

Composite evidence crosses the review threshold. Decision dock opens with destructive action clearly labeled, never auto-executed.

RULE_CONFLICT

Manual precedence

Two rules disagree. Show both rule sources, precedence options and the rationale required to resolve the conflict.

NEEDS_VERIFICATION

Step-up pending

Analyst defers the final decision until an out-of-band identity challenge succeeds, fails or expires.

FROZEN

Outbound rails locked

Account state is visibly frozen and downstream actions communicate what remains possible.

FALSE_POSITIVE

Baseline restored

Clear the alert, retain rationale and feed the disposition back into review history.

ESCALATED

Specialist handoff

Ownership changes without losing evidence, SLA context, notes or previous analyst actions.

02.06

Recovery states

When the happy path breaks, Sentry should preserve the investigation and make the next safe move obvious.

2FA FAILED

Challenge unsuccessful

Return to the same alert with the failed challenge attached to evidence; allow freeze or escalation.

2FA EXPIRED

Verification window closed

Mark the challenge expired; do not silently treat it as a customer failure.

SAVE ERROR

Audit commit failed

Do not auto-advance. Retain the analyst decision locally and offer a safe retry while preventing duplicate actions.
